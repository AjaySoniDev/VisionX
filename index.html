What remains

  • Causal live streaming and desktop-to-mobile inference parity.
  • Wearable camera transport, fault handling, power/enclosure validation.
  • Physical turn direction/timing contract and live route-progress logic.
  • Representative held-out benchmark, calibration and unknown-route rejection evidence.
  • Supervised validation with intended users and mobility specialists.

Claim discipline

Do not infer more than the evidence supports

Source code, passing tests and clean architecture are software-engineering evidence. They are not substitutes for route-recognition efficacy, accepted-error/coverage results, calibration, safe live guidance, or user benefit. Likewise, the proposed business model is a deployment hypothesis, not evidence of market demand.
