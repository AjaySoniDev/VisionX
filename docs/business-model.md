# Proposed business and deployment model

VisionX's proposed initial use case is taught-route confirmation on a controlled campus route, alongside the user's existing mobility tools. The one-junction/two-branch core does not supply general destination routing or physical turn instructions.

## Conditional path

1. Establish an actual unresolved user need, allowing the conclusion that a simpler phone-only or existing strategy is preferable.
2. Collect independent sessions and unknowns; compare methods fairly with accepted error and coverage.
3. Validate mobile descriptor/ranking parity, causal decisions, freshness, fault recovery and sustained resources.
4. Conduct accessible, consenting supervised co-design with relevant mobility expertise.
5. Consider an institution-supported pilot only if usefulness and complete support costs are demonstrated.

The current Pi/Android detection test is team-reported; it does not complete these route-assistance gates.

## Possible revenue, after validation

| Proposal | Possible payer | Required proof |
|---|---|---|
| Bounded evaluation engagement | Institution / research sponsor | Agreed scope, supervision and measurements |
| Supported kit or managed loan | Institution | Validated BOM, manufacturing, repair and service capacity |
| Enrollment and accessible training | Deployment sponsor | Measured staff effort and usable workflow |
| Maintenance agreement | Institution | Actual ongoing route revalidation/support needs |
| Later core SDK licensing | Integrator | Stable contracts, rights review and demand |

No price, customer demand, partnership, revenue, subsidy, government endorsement or cost-saving percentage is validated. Government procurement and CSR are possibilities to investigate, not entitlements or commitments.

## Cost framework

Account for the camera node, fitted sensor, cable, storage, mount, enclosure, safe regulated power, audio, compatible phone, assembly, testing, enrollment, accessibility training, support, repair, logistics and end-of-life data erasure. Separate owned/donated equipment from complete economic cost. Optional IMU and additional sensors have separate evidence/cost obligations.

Delivered kit cost = parts + assembly/testing + logistics + applicable charges + expected warranty cost. Deployment cost includes phone provision where required and measured support/enrollment effort. These are accounting relationships, not forecasts.

User benefit needs comprehension, completion, wrong-turn/intervention, workload, preference and withdrawal evidence. Environmental benefit requires a comparable lifecycle and energy assessment. Phone reuse or absence of installed markers alone proves neither affordability nor net environmental saving.
